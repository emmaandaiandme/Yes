Simple image hosting

Share images without the clutter.

Upload images directly from Discord and receive a clean, permanent share link. Files are kept across app restarts.

Learn how it works

Simple by design

1. Attach

Run /upload in Discord and attach one supported image.

2. Upload

The bot validates and stores images up to 20 MB. For larger images, use /more.

3. Share

Copy the immutable image URL and use it in Discord, chat, docs, or websites.

Built for quick sharing

Permanent links

Image metadata and files are stored outside the running process, so restarts do not erase them.

Supported formats

PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP, BMP, AVIF, and TIFF up to 20 MB in Discord, or 100 MB through the larger upload page.

Private by default

There are no public listings. Someone needs the exact image link to view an upload.
